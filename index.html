How it works

1. Fund

Creators deposit BOT or USDT and publish questions with a deadline.

2. Respond

One submission per wallet. Creators approve or reject with a reason; rejected wallets can appeal.

3. Close and finalize

Pool after fee ÷ valid submissions. Any remainder goes one unit each to the earliest approved respondents.

4. Claim and withdraw

Rewards accumulate across campaigns. Withdraw once you pass the minimum.

FAQ

When are rewards paid?

Only after the campaign is closed and finalized. Approved respondents then claim and withdraw.

What if nobody is approved?

The pool is refunded to the creator if the campaign was set up with that policy.

Is my response private?

No. Chain data is public. Use the hash-only option when you want a commitment instead of readable answers.
